Band (between the outer and inner lines)

Band width is the distance between the outer and inner outlines; the inner one is centred exactly inside the outer.

Edges

Fine stitch widths are always in millimetres. 1.5 mm of space is snug; 4 to 8 mm looks roomy.

5. Fabric

Pick a thread that matches your fabric. Until you pick one, StitchKit uses the design's first colour so nothing shows past the edges.

6. Stitching

Normal is tuned to the usual guide of about 1,500 stitches per covered square inch. Your fabric choice sets this; change it if you like.

7. Hooping

More than one: StitchKit picks the smallest hoop that holds them, 10 mm apart, and sews colour by colour (all of colour 1, then all of colour 2…).

8. File types

Every piece also gets a thread sheet, a placement template to print, and a Cricut cut file when there is appliqué.
